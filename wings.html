Outcasts Wings

The Outcasts community is growing. Explore our dedicated interest wings where you can connect with people who share your specific passions.

Not for academics, just for fun. Build side projects, participate in hackathons together, or just debate which programming language is supreme.

Weekly watch parties, movie breakdowns, and heated debates on whether the manga was actually better than the anime.
